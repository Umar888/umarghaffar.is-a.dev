About

Hi, I'm Umar

A mobile developer who likes owning a feature from the first requirements call through to the store release.

I started out as an Android intern at NetSol in 2018 and have been shipping mobile apps ever since. Today I'm a Senior Software Engineer at Xavor Corporation, building Flutter and native features for Guitar Center's commerce and in-store apps.

Along the way I've built Android POS systems, led an OTT streaming app with DRM, and delivered Flutter and React Native products for clients. I work closely with product, design, QA and client teams, mentor other developers, and turn business requirements into apps people can rely on.
