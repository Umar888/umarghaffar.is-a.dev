scroll_edge_blur

v0.1.1

Progressive, iOS style edge blur for scroll views, toolbars and headers. A GPU shader runs on the backdrop with no snapshots, so it stays cheap while scrolling.

liquid_glass_navigation

v0.1.1

iOS 26 style Liquid Glass navigation: a floating glass bottom bar, a glass side rail and an adaptive scaffold that switches between them, with a stretchy, draggable pill.

smart_signature

v0.1.0

A signature field that improves what you draw, entirely on-device: it removes jitter, pen hooks and stray marks, adds pen-like ink and levels the baseline.
